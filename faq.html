Getting started

Do I need my own guitar?

Not for the first lesson. We'll talk through what kind of guitar makes sense for your goals and budget before you buy anything. If you already have one, bring it — any guitar works for a beginner.

Getting started

Am I too old to learn?

Absolutely not. The majority of my students are adults who never played before. Adults actually have real advantages — focus, motivation, and the ability to understand music theory intuitively. There is no age limit.

Progress

How quickly will I see results?

Most students play their first song within the first two lessons. Within two months you'll have a handful of songs you can actually perform. Progress depends on practice — even 10 minutes a day makes a huge difference.

Scheduling

What times are available?

I offer morning, evening, and weekend slots to fit around school runs, work schedules, and everything in between. Available times shift week to week — the best way to find your slot is to book a free intro lesson and we'll sort it out from there.

Lessons

Online or in-person — which is better?

Both work really well. Online lessons are more flexible and many students prefer them. In-person allows me to correct hand position more directly, which can help beginners form good habits faster. Many students do both depending on the week.

Pricing

What's your cancellation policy?

Life happens — I get it. Cancel or reschedule anytime with at least 24 hours notice and there's no charge. Late cancellations are charged at half rate. I try to be as flexible as possible, and I just ask for the same in return.
