My story

From student to teacher

I picked up my first guitar at 14 and haven't put it down since. After studying music at university and spending years playing in bands and recording studios, I realized the thing I loved most wasn't performing — it was watching someone else discover they could actually play.

I started teaching formally twelve years ago, and in that time I've worked with students aged 7 to 72. My specialty is adult beginners — people who always wanted to learn but never found the right time or the right teacher.

That's where I come in.

Teaching philosophy

Progress over perfection

I don't care if your technique is textbook-perfect in week one. I care that you're excited to pick up your guitar tomorrow. We build good habits gradually, around songs you actually want to play.
