My story

From Listener to Player.

In 1987, I watched two counselors in a distant field figure out "Patience" by ear: a moment of pure magic that taught me music is about connection, not just notes. That spark led to my first guitar and fueled my mission to help students of all ages find their own voice. Today, I help you unlock that same "aha!" moment and bring your favorite music to life on the strings.

Teaching philosophy

Progress over perfection

I focus on keeping you excited about your progress rather than stressing over perfect technique in the first week. We build solid habits by starting with the music that moves you.

Theory with Purpose

Learning here is about having fun, not feeling like a classroom. We incorporate music theory as a secondary tool to help you understand what’s happening inside the songs you're learning. It’s designed to make you a smarter player, not to give you more homework.
