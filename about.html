My story

From Listener to Player.

In 1987, I watched two counselors in a distant field figure out "Patience" by ear—a moment of pure magic that taught me music is about connection, not just notes. That spark led to my first guitar and fueled my mission to help students of all ages find their own voice. Today, I help you (or your child) unlock that same "aha!" moment and bring your favorite music to life on the strings.

Teaching philosophy

Progress over perfection

I don't care if your technique is textbook-perfect in week one. I care that you're excited to pick up your guitar tomorrow. We build good habits gradually, around songs you actually want to play.
