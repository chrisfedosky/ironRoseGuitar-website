Privacy Policy - Iron Rose Guitar

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

1. Information Collection

When you reach out via the contact form on this website to inquire about lessons, I collect basic information necessary for scheduling and communication:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address (for correspondence)
  • Your phone number (if provided by you)
  • Any details regarding your guitar goals or interests.

2. Use of Information

I use the information provided solely for one purpose: to respond accurately, coordinate lesson times, and manage our relationship as teacher/student. Your details will never be used for external marketing campaigns or spam.

3. Data Security & Sharing

Your privacy is a priority, and I operate with maximum simplicity and security:

  • No Sales: Your personal data will never be sold, rented, or traded to any third party.
  • Private Management: All details remain securely managed by me for lesson administration only.
  • Security Measures: I utilize secure digital systems and practices to ensure your contact information remains private and protected.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to view, update, or request complete deletion of your contact information at any time. Simply send me a direct email inquiry and I will ensure all records are removed promptly.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact me directly through the website's inquiry form.
