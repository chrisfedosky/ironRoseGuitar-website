Privacy Policy - Iron Rose Guitar

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

1. Information I Collect

When you fill out the contact form on this site, I collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (if provided)
  • Any details you share about your guitar goals

2. How I Use Your Information

I use this information for one reason only: to answer your questions and coordinate your guitar lessons. I will never use your details for unexpected marketing or spam.

3. Data Protection and Sharing

Your privacy matters to me, so I keep things simple and secure:

  • No Selling: I never sell, rent, or trade your information with anyone else.
  • No Third Parties: Your details stay with me and are only used to manage our lessons.
  • Security: I use secure systems to ensure your contact form details stay private.

4. Your Rights

You can ask me to view, change, or completely delete your contact information at any time. Just send me an email, and I will remove it immediately.

5. Contact Me

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please reach out directly through the contact form on this website.
